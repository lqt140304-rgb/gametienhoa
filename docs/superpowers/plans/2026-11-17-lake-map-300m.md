# Underroot Lake 300 m Map Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Rebuild the first zone as a 300×300 m lake map laid out like the approved concept zoning map, with a root canopy and root rim, swimmable water, and a landmark per zone.

**Architecture:** `ZoneLayout` becomes the single data table (zones, lake outline, stream, pools, trails, in metres). Terrain, water, ground, flora, roots and landmarks all read it, as the Dress Lake tools already do by reflection. Swimming is pure logic (`SwimLogic`) used by `SlimeController`.

**Tech Stack:** Unity 6.6 URP, C# 9, NUnit EditMode tests, Netcode for GameObjects (position sync only).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-lake-map-300m-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- Map 300×300 m, origin at the centre, +z north. Zone centres (m): 01 (8,-80), 02 (-85,-15), 03 (-70,68), 06 (20,90), 04 (98,65), 05 (122,-10).
- Zone sizes (m, x×z): 01 105×88; 02 88×70; 03 105×88; 04 88×88; 05 122×53; 06 105×70.
- Water depths: life shallows 0.4, stream 0.15, two deep pools 2.0. All water the original teal (clean), never olive.
- Slime 0.6 m; root shelter slit 0.9 m wide × 0.65 m high, cavern roof 12 m; root tunnel 4 m × 8 m; rotting log Ø 1.5 m; gel nursery bank 2 m wide; mushroom landmark cluster 1.2 m.
- Canopy ~25 m high, 8–10 light shafts. No invisible blockers around pools.
- C# 9 (no struct field initialisers), no mutable statics without reset, hidden meshes `HideAndDontSave`, never `DestroyImmediate` in `OnValidate`. Editor tools stay idempotent. Nothing can be compiled here: say so, ask for Console screenshots.

## Review Focus
- Slime standing exactly at a pool rim or on the stream (0.15 m): must wade, not swim or fall through.
- Slime below the surface looking up/down: camera must not clip the water mesh unexpectedly.
- Rebuilding the lake twice: no duplicate objects, no duplicate carving.
- Remote (co-op) players in the water: position/rotation sync unchanged.
- Zone polygons overlapping each other or the lake: nothing spawns inside water.
