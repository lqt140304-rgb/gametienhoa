# Lake Map 500 m Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Grow the lake zone to 500 m with noise-shaped lake/stream/pools/zones, rolling terrain, blended living plant communities and swimming that looks like swimming.

**Architecture:** `ZoneLayout` stays the single source: zone influence fields, shoreline/depth functions and trails, all seeded noise. `TerrainShape` adds relief from it. A new `PlantCommunities` (pure) answers "which plant, how dense, here". Editor tools read these. Swim visuals: render order + float depth + ripple ring + underwater tint.

**Tech Stack:** Unity 6.6 URP, C# 9, NUnit EditMode.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-lake-map-500m-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- Map 500 × 500 m, `WorldScale.ZoneSize = 500`, heightmap 1025. Zone centres: 01 (-8,-120), 02 (-150,-18), 03 (-125,118), 04 (137,110), 05 (173,-8), 06 (13,153); B entry (17,233). Sizes 01 80×70, 02 70×60, 03 80×70, 04 70×70, 05 90×40, 06 80×55.
- Lake around (-8,-42), ~220×160 m, noisy shore; middle 1.2–1.8 m, shoals 0.3–0.4 m, bay 0.4 m toward 01. Stream (213,10)→(187,-7)→(158,-17)→(133,-25)→(103,-37), width 7–12 m, 0.15 m deep. Pools r≈10 (noisy), 2 m, at (193,0) and (157,-20). Puddle (-120,103) r 6, 0.3 m, dirty yellow-green. All other water original teal.
- Relief: in zones ±1–1.5 m; between zones up to ~6 m; rim cliff from ±175 m to ~20 m at ±250 m, gap at the B entry; walkable ≤ ~35° except the rim.
- Zone influence 1 at core → 0 over ~60 m, noise-warped border. Swim: depth ≥ 0.5 m, Space up / Ctrl down, 65 % speed, light energy cost, no drowning; body centre floats 0.2 m below the surface.
- C# 9; no Unity here (user runs Rebuild + Test Runner); tools idempotent; place by real ground height.

## Review Focus
- Shore at grazing angles: slime must wade (not swim) where depth < 0.5 m even right beside 0.6 m.
- Two zone influences equal at a point: plant choice must still be deterministic and blended, never empty.
- Trails crossing water only at the stream (fords ≤ 0.15 m).
- Rebuild twice → same terrain, same plants (seeded), no duplicates.
- Camera exactly at the water level: no flicker between underwater tint on/off (hysteresis 0.05 m).
